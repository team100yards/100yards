How Our Software Ecosystem Works Together

Our two applications are designed to work together seamlessly or as standalone solutions, providing a complete 360-degree technology stack for football academies, competitive clubs, and tournament directors.

1. Preparation & Training

Coaches use the Coach Management System during the week to schedule training sessions, review video telestrations, analyze player fitness, and fine-tune team formations.

2. Matchday & Tournament

On matchday, tournament organizers and officials run the Event Managing System to log official team sheets, broadcast live scorelines, update group standings, and manage brackets.

3. Post-Match Review & AI Debrief

After the final whistle, coaches import match events into the AI-Powered Match Report Studio to produce instant performance feedback and player development notes.
